## QSB TEA
QSB (quantitative/quantum systems/synthetic biology/biotech) tea is a friendly and casual tea hour to facilitate inter-disciplinary conversations amongst biologists, chemists, physicists, bioengineers, chemical engineers, computer engineers, etc, under the common goal of understanding and engineering complex biological systems to solve the challenges our society faces. We are proudly sponsored by 凤凰生命, an early stage venture capital based in Hangzhou focusing on long-term biotech trends.
