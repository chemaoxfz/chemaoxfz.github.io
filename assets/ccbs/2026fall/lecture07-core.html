Equilibrium is a local privilege

A living cell can use equilibrium models locally. Free energy becomes necessary only when a maintained current buys a named behavior that equilibrium topology forbids or sharply limits.

CCBS · EST 5053 · Fall 2026 · lecture 7 · core page (full) · 2026-09-22 (Tue) 10:40–12:15 · students present, lecturer-backed

“The cell is far from equilibrium” is true but not a model. Ligand binding may relax in milliseconds while transcription changes over minutes, even though the larger cell continuously consumes fuel. This lecture first earns an equilibrium description for a fast local subnetwork. It then opens the state graph, asks whether probability circulates around any cycle, and spends free energy only where that circulation creates a measurable function.

The deliverable

By minute 95, a team should be able to enumerate equilibrium states, normalize their weights, distinguish stationarity from detailed balance, calculate a cycle affinity, and complete an energy audit that states the equilibrium prohibition, driven benefit, current, cost, and alternative explanation.

Part 1

Earn equilibrium locally

State weights become legitimate only after the system boundary, relaxation time, and observable have been named.

1Put a box around the claim

Equilibrium is local in components, time, and observable.

Draw a box around the binding or conformational subnetwork. Outside the box, ligand concentrations, ATP, and other reservoirs may be maintained by the cell. Inside it, ask whether the selected states exchange much faster than synthesis, degradation, or measurement. If so, their occupancies may relax before the environment appreciably changes.

This does not claim that the whole cell is closed or lifeless. It says that a particular conditional distribution can be approximated by equilibrium weights. The approximation fails when a fuel-driven transition crosses the box, when a cycle carries resolved current, or when the experiment watches the transient rather than the relaxed occupancy.1

The boundary also determines what “no work” means. A receptor subnetwork may exchange no net work internally while the cell spends energy elsewhere to hold ligand, ion, or nucleotide concentrations fixed. Those chemostatted species belong on the diagram. Removing them from view can make a driven open system look like a closed equilibrium one.

Room check, 60 seconds. Your receptor exchanges ligand every 0.01 s, while the reporter matures in 8 min. What can plausibly equilibrate? What cannot be inferred from the reporter alone?

2Build the state ledger

An equilibrium probability is one state weight divided by the sum of all allowed weights.

For state ii with free energy GiG_i, assign a weight wi=e−βGiw_i=e^{-\beta G_i} after concentration factors and standard states are included. Then

pi=wiZ,Z=∑jwj,β=(kBT)−1.p_i=\frac{w_i}{Z},\qquad Z=\sum_jw_j,\qquad \beta=(k_BT)^{-1}. (1)

A receptor that is empty or ligand-bound has weights 11 and c/Kdc/K_d, so pbound=c/(Kd+c)p_{\rm bound}=c/(K_d+c). The denominator is the ledger of mutually exclusive possibilities. Omitting a state changes the model.

Concentrations enter logarithms as dimensionless activity ratios, such as μ=μ∘+kBTln⁡(c/c∘)\mu=\mu^\circ+k_BT\ln(c/c^\circ). This corrects the 2025 shortcut that converted a single hydrogen-bond energy directly into a universal affinity factor. Solvation, entropy, conformational reorganization, and standard state all contribute to the measured binding free energy.2

3Let binding reshape an ensemble

Cooperativity can emerge because a ligand selects between conformations, not because ligands touch one another.

In the Monod–Wyman–Changeux model, an oligomer occupies two concerted conformations with different ligand affinities.3 For nn equivalent sites, representative weights are (1+c/KR)n(1+c/K_R)^n and L(1+c/KT)nL(1+c/K_T)^n. Their normalized ratio gives the active-state probability.

Increasing site number can sharpen the population shift, but an effective Hill slope does not simply count binding sites. It depends on the allosteric equilibrium, affinity ratio, and concentration interval used for the fit. A successful occupancy model predicts how leak, midpoint, dynamic range, and slope move together under interventions. Fitting one sigmoid is weaker evidence.

A sharp classroom test is to perturb one measured ingredient rather than refit the whole curve. Change ligand affinity, repressor copy number, or conformational bias, carry that number through the same partition function, and predict the new response. Agreement across such interventions is what turns the ledger from a descriptive fit into a physical model.

Part 2

Test the graph

Occupancies alone do not identify equilibrium. Edges and cycles expose the missing currents.

4Separate three balances

Stationarity cancels node totals. Detailed balance cancels every edge current.

For a continuous-time Markov chain, a stationary distribution satisfies QTπ=0Q^T\pi=0. Equivalently, total inflow equals total outflow at every node. Detailed balance is stronger:

global: ∑jπjkji=πi∑jkij,detailed: πikij=πjkji for every edge.\text{global: }\sum_j\pi_jk_{ji}=\pi_i\sum_jk_{ij},\qquad \text{detailed: }\pi_i k_{ij}=\pi_j k_{ji}\ \text{for every edge}. (2)

The 2025 scribe note labels the node equation “detailed balance.” That is the central correction. A stationary ring can maintain clockwise current while every node occupancy stays constant. “Ergodic,” “stationary,” “equilibrium,” and “Boltzmann” answer different questions.4

5Find the cycles

A closed tree has no route for stationary circulation. A loop can carry one.

At a leaf of a finite tree, stationarity forces the current on its only edge to zero. Remove that leaf and repeat. Every edge current vanishes. This is why a closed two-state switch can look equilibrium-like at stationarity even when its two rates are unequal.

A cycle has no leaf. Detailed balance on a loop requires the product of forward rates to equal the product of reverse rates:

∏cyclekforward=∏cyclekreverse.\prod_{\rm cycle}k_{\rm forward}=\prod_{\rm cycle}k_{\rm reverse}. (3)

The equality must hold on every independent cycle. Linear versus nonlinear is not the deciding distinction. Topology, closure, and reservoir coupling are.

6Measure affinity and current

Drive is a potential difference. Dissipation also requires flow.

Define the dimensionless cycle affinity

A=ln⁡∏kforward∏kreverse.A=\ln\frac{\prod k_{\rm forward}}{\prod k_{\rm reverse}}. (4)

When local detailed balance applies, AA is tied to a chemical-potential drop such as ATP hydrolysis. For a single cycle, entropy production is proportional to net current times affinity, JAJA. Large affinity across a blocked edge can carry little current. Rapid traffic near balance can have large gross flux but small net dissipation.

Current on edge i⇌ji\rightleftharpoons j is Jij=πikij−πjkjiJ_{ij}=\pi_i k_{ij}-\pi_j k_{ji}. At detailed balance every such difference is zero. Away from it, node balance still makes currents continuous around a simple loop. This is why a cycle diagram plus stationary occupancies and rates is enough to expose direction, whereas occupancies alone are not.

In the generated three-state ring, accelerating one forward edge raises current at first and then saturates because another edge becomes limiting. Spending more free energy on the already-fast step produces diminishing functional return.

Part 3

Name what energy buys

A nonequilibrium claim is incomplete until a forbidden equilibrium behavior becomes a measured benefit.

7Unlock a forbidden response

Drive matters when it changes the family of attainable input-output functions.

Consider a four-state promoter square: empty, transcription-factor bound, polymerase bound, and both bound. With zero or one transcription factor per state, equilibrium weights make polymerase occupancy a fractional-linear function, (a+bx)/(c+dx)(a+bx)/(c+dx). Its derivative has one sign. It cannot have an interior maximum.

The generated driven network has cycle affinity 4.166 kBT4.166\,k_BT. Its output rises from 0.158, peaks at 0.396 near input 0.365, and falls to 0.178. The number is not a universal price. It is a witness: the driven topology realizes a response shape excluded by that equilibrium state model. Different driven edges can reach the boundary at very different cost.5

8Proofread with a reset loop

A fuel-coupled intermediate gives a wrong substrate another chance to leave before product forms.

Hopfield's proofreading mechanism inserts a delayed, driven state between initial binding and product.6 Wrong substrates dissociate more often during the extra wait. The reset loop prevents the scheme from simply equilibrating through the same states.

The gain is not “one ATP buys one decimal place.” Error, product flux, and resets per product move together as rates change. In the generated five-state example, increasing reset improves discrimination but eventually suppresses total throughput and raises fuel use. A proofreading claim must report all three outputs and show where discrimination occurs.

Specificity alone is not proof of kinetic proofreading. Structural mismatch can also improve discrimination, and repeated equilibrium binding can create long effective delays. Resolve intermediate dwell times, perturb the fuel-coupled step, and test whether the error changes as the proposed reset network predicts.

9Count independent samples

Energy can reset a sensor and shorten correlations, but it cannot turn one random arrival into many independent observations.

Berg and Purcell related sensing error to diffusion, receptor geometry, concentration, and observation time.7 At its statistical core, NN independent events give fractional uncertainty proportional to N−1/2N^{-1/2}. Doubling precision requires four times as many independent samples.

A driven receptor cycle may release ligand, adapt, or reuse a sensor faster. That can increase effective sample count in a fixed time. It does not erase counting noise without changing samples, time, geometry, transport, or prior information. Energy and time are resources, not interchangeable currencies with a universal exchange rate.

10Audit the energy claim

Use equilibrium where it passes, and add the smallest driven cycle that earns a named function.

  1. Boundary: draw the modeled subnetwork and mark chemostatted species.
  2. Observable: define the output as an occupancy, edge flux, error, time, or noise statistic.
  3. Equilibrium baseline: enumerate weights and solve it first.
  4. Prohibition: prove which behavior the state count or topology excludes.
  5. Drive: identify independent cycles and calculate affinities.
  6. Flow: solve stationary occupancies and currents.
  7. Benefit and cost: report function with dissipation, delay, and noise.
  8. Challenge: vary driven rates until the advantage disappears.

Homework 6 supplies the raw cases: allostery, a four-state promoter, and stochastic binding.8 Their common question is now explicit. If the claimed function survives after every cycle affinity is set to zero while preserving the same states and observables, dissipation was not necessary for that claim.

The arrival

Equilibrium is not the opposite of life. It is a local privilege earned when fast state exchange erases current from the observable. Free energy becomes explanatory only when the current it maintains is drawn, measured, and connected to something the corresponding equilibrium graph cannot do.
